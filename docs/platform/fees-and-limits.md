# Fees & Limits

Fees, exchange rates, and limits depend on the corridor, asset, payout type, and account or compliance profile. They can change as product and local-rail conditions change.

## What to Expect

Before a recipient confirms a wallet or settlement action, Fiatsend presents the applicable information for that action. Businesses receive their commercial terms during onboarding.

## Collecting Payments

Payments made with **Fiatsend checkout** (payment links, invoices and website checkout) carry a collection fee, currently **0.5%** of the payment. You choose whether you absorb it or add it to what your customer pays, as a default in [Payment settings](/docs/payments/payment-settings) and per payment link.

Payments your customers make by **mobile money** or **bank transfer** go straight to your own account and aren't processed by Fiatsend, so the collection fee doesn't apply to them.

Payment links stay open for up to 7 days, and the exchange rate for a cedi-priced link is locked when it's created. Your commercial agreement takes precedence over this page.

## Get Current Terms

- **Recipients:** review the details presented in the Fiatsend wallet before you confirm.
- **Businesses:** use your commercial agreement and Fiatsend Console, then contact your Fiatsend representative for questions.
- **Developers:** consult [developer.fiatsend.com](https://developer.fiatsend.com) for API responses and integration behavior; it is not a substitute for commercial terms.
