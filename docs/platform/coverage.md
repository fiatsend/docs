# Coverage

Fiatsend's live launch market is Ghana. Product availability—including recipient settlement methods, asset support, limits, and onboarding requirements—can vary by corridor, provider, account type, and compliance profile.

## Confirm Availability Before Launch

Do not use this site as a real-time availability or pricing feed. Before you launch or advertise Fiatsend in a location, confirm with your Fiatsend contact:

- The recipient country and supported local delivery options.
- Asset and conversion availability.
- Account verification requirements and applicable limits.
- Any provider maintenance or corridor restrictions.

Businesses integrating with Fiatsend should use [Developer Documentation](https://developer.fiatsend.com) for the current API-supported networks and limits.
