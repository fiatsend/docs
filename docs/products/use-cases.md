# Business Use Cases

Fiatsend helps platforms make recipient-first payouts: funds are delivered to a Fiatsend wallet, where the recipient can hold them or use an available local settlement option.

## Payroll and Employer-of-Record Platforms

Pay contractors and employees without making local payout operations a separate product. Recipients receive funds in their Fiatsend wallet and can manage settlement on their own schedule.

## Marketplaces and Gig Platforms

Pay sellers, drivers, and service providers when work is complete. New recipients can onboard after the payout is created, so a missing pre-existing account does not make the payment disappear.

## Exchanges and Wallets

Offer eligible customers a compliant route from stablecoin value to a Fiatsend wallet, with local settlement options managed by the recipient.

## Online Stores, SaaS and Subscriptions

Add a [payment button](/docs/payments/website-checkout) to a product page, or create a checkout from your server for every order. Customers pay with Fiatsend checkout, mobile money or bank transfer, and your system is told by a signed webhook when each order is paid.

## Freelancers, Agencies and Service Businesses

Send a [branded invoice](/docs/payments/invoices) by email or WhatsApp, follow it from sent to paid, and let Fiatsend send the receipt. Customers can pay online or by mobile money or bank transfer.

## Shops and Vendors

Share a [payment link or QR code](/docs/payments/payment-links) for a single sale, or show a terminal QR code for in-person checkout.

## Before You Go Live

Every business must complete Fiatsend onboarding and satisfy corridor-specific requirements. Start with the [Fiatsend Console guide](/docs/products/fiatsend-console): create an account, unlock sandbox, connect a Stellar wallet on testnet, and run a payment-link, invoice, website checkout and payout dry run before promising a production experience.

Confirm product fit, supported locations, settlement methods, and production access with Fiatsend before going live. For API implementation details, start at [developer.fiatsend.com](https://developer.fiatsend.com).
