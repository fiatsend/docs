# Fiatsend Wallet

The Fiatsend wallet is where recipients receive and manage payouts sent through Fiatsend. It is accessed through [app.fiatsend.com](https://app.fiatsend.com).

## What Recipients Can Do

- Receive a payout from a participating business.
- Review payment status and available balance.
- Hold funds in the wallet.
- Use an available local settlement option, or configure automatic settlement where that option is available.

## New Recipients

A Fiatsend account is not required before a sender creates a payout. If the recipient is new, Fiatsend sends a secure onboarding link. The recipient follows the link, completes the required account and verification steps, and then accesses the funds.

Never share a verification code, password, or recovery phrase with anyone. Fiatsend support will not ask for them.

## Getting Help

For a payment you do not recognize, a missing onboarding link, or an account issue, use the contact paths in [Support](/docs/resources/support).
