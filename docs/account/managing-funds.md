# Managing Funds

When a business pays you through Fiatsend, the payment is delivered to your Fiatsend wallet. Your wallet activity shows the status of each payment and the balance you can manage.

## Receiving a Payment

1. Open the secure Fiatsend link sent to you, or sign in at [app.fiatsend.com](https://app.fiatsend.com).
2. Complete any required onboarding or verification.
3. Confirm that the payout appears in your wallet activity.
4. Choose to hold the funds or use an available settlement option.

If automatic settlement is enabled for your account, Fiatsend initiates the selected local delivery method after a payout arrives. Availability and processing time can vary by corridor and provider.

## Payment Status

Use wallet activity as the authoritative view of a recipient payment. If a status stays unchanged longer than expected, contact the business that sent the payment first and then [Fiatsend Support](/docs/resources/support) with the payment reference, if you have one.

## Keep Your Account Safe

- Use only links and apps you expect to be from Fiatsend.
- Do not share one-time codes or account credentials.
- Check recipient and settlement details before confirming an action.
- Report suspected fraud immediately through [Support](/docs/resources/support).

For developers creating payouts, use the [Developer Documentation](https://developer.fiatsend.com), not this guide.
