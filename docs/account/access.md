# Account Access

Use [app.fiatsend.com](https://app.fiatsend.com) to access a recipient wallet and [console.fiatsend.com](https://console.fiatsend.com) for a business account.

## Recipient Wallet

If you receive a payout and do not yet have an account, follow the secure onboarding link sent to you. You may be asked to verify information before you can access all features or settlement options.

If you already have an account, sign in through the official Fiatsend app. Do not use links sent by an unknown person, and never give a verification code to someone else.

## Business Access

1. Open [console.fiatsend.com](https://console.fiatsend.com) and register with a company-managed email.
2. Wait for sandbox activation (**verified** status), then sign in.
3. Follow [Get started in Fiatsend Console](/docs/products/fiatsend-console#get-started) for wallet connect, [collecting payments](/docs/payments/overview), and payouts.
4. Invite teammates from **Team**. Each person should use an individual account.

Ask your organization administrator to add or remove access. For production (`active` status), complete Go live / KYB in Console Settings.

## Trouble Signing In

Start with the account-recovery steps on the sign-in screen. If you believe someone else has accessed your account, contact [Support](/docs/resources/support) as soon as possible.
