# Support

Use the route that matches your issue so Fiatsend can help without exposing your account information.

| Need help with | First step |
|---|---|
| A payment you received or your wallet | Sign in to [Fiatsend](https://app.fiatsend.com) and use the in-app support option. |
| A payment link, invoice or checkout from a business | Contact the business that sent it first. Replies to invoice and receipt emails go to them. |
| A payout sent by a business | Contact the business that sent it, then provide Fiatsend with the payout reference if needed. |
| Business onboarding or account access | Contact your Fiatsend representative or use [Fiatsend Console](https://console.fiatsend.com). |
| Website checkout, API, SDK, webhook, or sandbox integration | Start with the [Website Checkout guide](/docs/payments/website-checkout) and [developer.fiatsend.com](https://developer.fiatsend.com). |
| Security or suspected fraud | Contact Fiatsend promptly through the app or your business support route. |

## Before You Contact Support

Include the payment or payout reference, the date and approximate amount, and a description of what you expected to happen. Do not send passwords, one-time codes, private keys, or full identity documents by email or chat.

## For Developers

The [Developer Documentation](https://developer.fiatsend.com) is the authoritative resource for API behavior. If you need help after following it, contact the technical support route provided during your business onboarding.
