# Rewards & Community

Fiatsend rewards active participants — users, agents, and merchants — through referral bonuses, in-app missions, and loyalty tiers. The rewards program is designed to grow the Fiatsend network organically while giving back to the people who use and promote the platform.

## Rewards Program

The Fiatsend rewards program has three pillars: referrals, missions, and loyalty.

### Referral Bonuses

Every Fiatsend user gets a unique referral link accessible from **Settings → Referral** in the [FiatsendOne app](https://app.fiatsend.com). When someone signs up using your link and completes their first transaction, both you and the new user earn a bonus.

| Aspect | Details |
|---|---|
| **Referral bonus (referrer)** | [TBD] per successful referral |
| **Welcome bonus (new user)** | [TBD] credited after first transaction |
| **Qualification** | New user must complete at least one transaction within 30 days of signup |
| **Payout method** | Credited directly to your Fiatsend wallet as GHSFIAT or stablecoin |
| **Limit** | No cap on referrals — earn for every qualified signup |

#### How It Works

1. Share your referral link via SMS, WhatsApp, social media, or any channel.
2. The new user clicks the link and creates a Fiatsend account.
3. The new user completes their first qualifying transaction (conversion, payout, or payment).
4. Both the referrer and the new user receive their bonus automatically.

:::tip
Agents and merchants with large networks can significantly boost their earnings through referrals. Combine referral outreach with in-person onboarding for the highest conversion rates.
:::

### In-App Missions

Missions are small, time-limited tasks that encourage users to explore platform features. Completing a mission earns points or direct bonuses. Examples of missions include:

- **First Payout** — Complete your first mobile money payout and earn bonus points.
- **Verify Your Identity** — Upgrade your [MobileNumber NFT](/docs/platform/mobilenumber-nft) to Level 1 or Level 2.
- **Invite 3 Friends** — Refer three users who each complete a transaction.
- **Merchant First Sale** — Accept your first QR payment as a merchant.
- **Weekly Active** — Make at least one transaction per week for four consecutive weeks.

Missions are refreshed periodically. Check the **Rewards** tab in the FiatsendOne app to see currently active missions and your progress.

## Community Channels

Fiatsend maintains active community channels where users, developers, agents, and merchants can connect, ask questions, share feedback, and stay informed about platform updates.

### X (Twitter) Spaces

Fiatsend hosts regular X Spaces sessions covering topics like platform updates, partner announcements, financial literacy, and open Q&A with the team. Follow [@fiaborEric](https://twitter.com/fiaborEric) and the Fiatsend accounts for announcements about upcoming Spaces.

### Webinars

Periodic webinars dive deeper into specific topics like:

- **Developer integration workshops** — Hands-on walkthroughs of the [Fiatsend API](/docs/api/overview) with live coding.
- **Agent training sessions** — Operational best practices for cash-in/out facilitation, reconciliation, and merchant onboarding.
- **Product demos** — Previews of upcoming features and how they'll impact your workflow.

Webinar recordings are shared in the community channels after each event.

### Partner Showcases

Fiatsend periodically highlights partners and integrators who are building interesting use cases on the platform. If you've built something noteworthy with Fiatsend — a payroll integration, a merchant POS setup, a custom mobile app — reach out to the team for a potential showcase feature.

## Feedback & Feature Requests

Fiatsend actively incorporates user feedback into its product roadmap. There are several ways to share your ideas:

| Channel | Best For |
|---|---|
| **In-app feedback** | Quick suggestions, bug reports, UX improvements |
| **Community channels** | Discussion and upvoting of feature ideas |
| **dev@fiatsend.com** | Technical feature requests, API enhancements |
| **contact@fiatsend.com** | General product feedback, partnership inquiries |

Feature requests are triaged by the product team and prioritized based on user impact, alignment with the platform roadmap, and technical feasibility.

## Campaign Calendar

Fiatsend runs monthly promotional campaigns targeted at different user segments — consumers, agents, and merchants. These campaigns offer bonus rewards, reduced fees, or exclusive access to new features.

Examples of past and recurring campaigns:

| Campaign | Target | Description |
|---|---|---|
| **New User Week** | Consumers | Enhanced welcome bonus for signups during the campaign period |
| **Agent Power Month** | Agents | Bonus commissions on cash-in/out transactions |
| **Merchant Growth Sprint** | Merchants | Reduced payout fees for merchants processing above a volume threshold |
| **Referral Rally** | All users | Double referral bonuses for a limited time |

:::info
Check the **Rewards** tab in the [FiatsendOne app](https://app.fiatsend.com) for current active campaigns. Campaign terms, bonus amounts, and eligibility requirements are always displayed within the app before participation.
:::
